Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

ZENVIO is a utility application that provides calculation, currency conversion and other everyday tools.

Information and app usage

The app may use Google Firebase Analytics to collect app usage events, such as app launches and feature interactions, to help understand and improve app performance. Analytics information may include app instance identifiers, device/app information and usage events as handled by Google Firebase. Do not enter sensitive personal information into analytics fields.

Advertising

ZENVIO may display advertisements through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process information as described in Google's applicable privacy disclosures and settings.

Third-party services

Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob are third-party services provided by Google. Their data handling is governed by Google's policies. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

Children

ZENVIO is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the ZENVIO app publisher through the project's GitHub repository.

This policy should be reviewed and updated if the app's data collection or third-party services change.
